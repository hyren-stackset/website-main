CAPABILITIES

Everything between commit and cloud

One platform for the whole lifecycle of your infrastructure — no glue scripts, no tribal knowledge, no 2 a.m. surprises.

Declarative stacks

Describe your entire environment in a single versioned file. Stackset reconciles reality to match — continuously.

Preview environments

Every pull request gets a production-grade clone with its own URL. Review changes running, not imagined.

Drift detection

Out-of-band change? Stackset flags it within seconds, shows the diff, and offers a one-click reconcile.

Cost telemetry

See the dollar impact of every change before you merge it. Budgets, forecasts, and per-stack breakdowns built in.

Zero-trust secrets

Short-lived credentials minted at deploy time and injected at runtime. Nothing sensitive ever sits at rest.

Instant rollbacks

Every deploy is an immutable snapshot. Restore any environment to any point in time in under a minute.
